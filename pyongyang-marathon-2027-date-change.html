Pyongyang Marathon 2027
has a new date!

Runners, dust off those calendars. The race is moving from spring to autumn.
The big news

April is out. October is in.

Our partners at the Pyongyang International Marathon Committee have let us know the 2027 race will not take place in April. It is almost certain to happen on October 3rd 2027 instead. It is not 100% locked in just yet, but it is highly likely, and we will shout about it as soon as it is.
What we know

Same race, new season

As the exclusive partner of the Pyongyang International Marathon, we heard it straight from the source. The race is shifting to the autumn, and all four distances are back on the menu:

Full Marathon
Half Marathon
10km
5km
 
Hold your horses (and your running shoes)
There is nothing to book or reserve yet. Trip packages, prices, the race route, time limits, capacity and eligibility are all still to be confirmed. Please don't try to book anything for now. We'll share every detail the moment we have it.

We expect demand to be high, probably higher than the number of places. Priority goes to those who booked the (sadly cancelled) 2026 Marathon and left a deposit. After that, places will open up once we know what packages are on offer.

For now, the only news is the new date. So pop October 3rd in the diary, keep training, and leave the rest to us!
