A note from Koryo Tours

Our apologies for the timing of our recent Nepal email

We want to acknowledge the timing of our recent newsletter announcing our new Nepal tour, which went out around the same time as the flooding in Nepal. The news is horrific, and the situation remains ongoing with much uncertainty.

Unfortunately, all we can do is apologise and explain what happened. Our newsletters go out via a ‘smart-sending’ function, which delivers a campaign automatically across a 24–48 hour window unique to each subscriber, based on when they're most likely to open it — and campaigns are often queued up several days in advance. We were able to stop our social media marketing (Facebook, Instagram, etc.), which was due out on the Thursday, but our newsletter is usually sent a little earlier, and the campaign had already begun sending before the flooding hit. Due to our own mistake, we were not able to cancel it in time.

We're so sorry this reached you at such a difficult time. Please know we're not taking the issue lightly, and we always strive to stay mindful in our marketing materials when it comes to more sensitive countries, especially around recent events.

Our apologies once again. We will certainly be more mindful with our automatic sending campaigns going forward.

Best,

The Koryo Tours Team
