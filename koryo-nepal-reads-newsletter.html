Something New
in Nepal

Fresh stories and travel guides from the Kathmandu Valley, and a first look at the brand-new trip they inspired.

Most people think of Nepal and picture boots, backpacks and Base Camp. We get it. But our team has been spending time in the valleys below the big peaks, and honestly, that’s where we fell for the place.

Potters at their wheels in Bhaktapur. Oil mills in Newar villages that haven’t changed in centuries. Falling asleep to chanting in a hilltop monastery. Learning to fold the perfect momo.

We’ve written it all up. Here’s what’s new on the Koryo site.
