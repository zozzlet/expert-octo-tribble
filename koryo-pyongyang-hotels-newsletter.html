Yanggakdo Hotel on Yanggak Island
Simon’s home-from-home

Yanggakdo International Hotel

47 floors on an island in the Taedong River, and the first place Simon ever stayed in the DPRK. Also the scene of the Great Fire Door Incident. (Yes, he kicked it down. Yes, it worked.)
 Pro-tip: The lobby beer is brewed on site. More than 10% foam? Send it back.

Oddest of all
 
📷 IMAGE
Kobangsan Hotel 70s basement
Saved the weirdest for last

Kobangsan Hotel

Built for VIPs, 30 minutes out of town and barely known even to guides. Upstairs is sleek 2015. The basement is pure 1970s: pool, one-lane bowling alley, and ping pong wallpaper so yellow you can’t actually play.
 Bonus: Suites come with the complete memoirs of Kim Il Sung. Take that, Netflix.
