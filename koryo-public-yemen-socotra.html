Mainland Yemen & Socotra Combo

Cairo · Seiyun · Shibam · Wadi Doan · Mukalla · Socotra · Aden

23 Jan – 6 Feb 15 Days / 14 Nights Small Group

The whole story, start to finish — and the trip we're proudest of. A week on the mainland through the mud-brick civilisation of the Hadhramaut: Shibam's 40-metre towers, the sacred desert town of Qabr Hud, the canyon villages of Wadi Doan, and two nights at a resort perched on a boulder 150 metres above the valley floor (infinity pool included). Then a direct flight to Socotra for a full island circuit by 4x4 — dragon blood forests, turquoise lagoons, dune-backed beaches and camping under some of the clearest night skies on Earth — before a grand finale in Aden.

The clever bit: routing through the mainland means just one Yemeni visa covers everything — the Socotra visa is waived for domestic arrivals. Visa and security clearance are included and arranged by us, with police escorts on all mainland ground transport and a Koryo Tours tour leader throughout.

Not your average itinerary

Travel that gets you closer

Landscapes are only half the story. Our itineraries are built around the people who live in them — experiences we've arranged directly with local communities, which is why you won't find them on any other tour.

  Honey tasting at the hive — Hadhramaut Sidr honey is considered among the finest in the world. We taste it with a Wadi Doan beekeeper who'll show you why it's prized across the Arab world.
  A school visit on Socotra — in the island capital of Hadibo, we meet children learning English at a local school, and drop into a women-run craft centre keeping Socotri traditions alive.
  Coffee in Yemeni homes — famous Yemeni coffee taken the proper way: on the floor, with local families in Seiyun and the villages of Wadi Doan. Lunch is served the same way, in a traditional local restaurant.
  Dress the part — every traveller receives a complimentary traditional Yemeni outfit on arrival, and evenings at the cliff-top Haid Al-Jazil resort can end with live Yemeni music and dance.
  The details in between — a camel-powered sesame press on the mountain road to Mukalla, dawn at the fish markets, dragon blood resin harvesting demonstrations at Homhil, and fresh seafood cooked by our own chef team on Socotra's beaches.
