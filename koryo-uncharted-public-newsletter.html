001

Egypt: Total Solar Eclipse 2027

Our very first Uncharted is monumental. Keep your eyes on the skies: it's an adventure fit for a pharaoh.
6+ minutes
of total darkness
On 2nd August 2027, the Moon swallows the Sun over North Africa. The Eclipse of the Century is the longest total eclipse visible from easily accessible land this century, and Egypt has the best seat in the house. While the crowds pile into Luxor, we'll be out in the desert near Asyut, deep in the path of totality, the Sun almost directly overhead and barely a cloud in sight.
Then we follow the sun through the temples of the pharaohs who worshipped it.

004  /  The Koryo twist

A healthy dollop of North Korea

It wouldn't be Koryo without it. Egypt has a DPRK story almost nobody knows, and we're spending a whole day on it.
6th of October Panorama
Built in 1989 with North Korean artists and architects. If you've been to Pyongyang's war museum, you'll recognise it instantly: a rotating hall around one giant battle painting.
The National Military Museum
Renovated by North Korean teams in the early 90s and packed with Socialist Realist dioramas. Look closely: many of the Egyptian soldiers have unmistakably East Asian faces.
Egypt & the Two Koreas
A Korean dinner in Zamalek with the story of an unlikely friendship: Kim Il Sung backing Nasser, North Korean pilots flying for Egypt in 1973, and the Egyptian company that built the DPRK's mobile network.
Where Korea was promised freedom
Our farewell dinner is at Mena House, where the 1943 Cairo Declaration promised Korea its independence. Taught in every Korean school, North and South. Pyramids lit up beyond the garden.
Plus the stories other tours leave out
●The Comet of 1882. Photographed beside the eclipsed Sun at Sohag for about 70 seconds, then never seen again. We get over six minutes.
●The Cairo Tower. Built, the story goes, with money the CIA offered Nasser. We watch the sunset from the top.
●Akhenaten's city of the sun. Tell el-Amarna, the capital of a pharaoh who worshipped only the sun disc. Very few tours make it here.
●Egypt's oldest standing obelisk. Nearly 4,000 years old, in a Cairo suburb most visitors drive straight past.
