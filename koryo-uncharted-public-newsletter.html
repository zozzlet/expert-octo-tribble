002  /  Uncharted Expedition 001

003  /  Not your usual Egypt

Five reasons this trip is pure Koryo

01
Darkness at midday
Six-plus minutes of totality with the Sun almost overhead, Venus and Mercury popping out beside it, and a celebration dinner on the darkest night of the month.
 
02
Pyongyang, by way of Cairo
Egypt's grand war Panorama and military museum were built and painted by North Korean artists. Spot the familiar style (and the soldiers with suspiciously East Asian faces).
 
03
The pharaoh who bet it all on the Sun
Akhenaten's lost capital at Tell el-Amarna, plus Abydos, Sohag and the city where Egypt's sun cult began. Few tours ever bother.
 
04
A tower built on CIA money
Or so the story goes. Sunset from the top of the Cairo Tower, Pyramids on the horizon.
 
05
Where Korea was promised freedom
A farewell dinner at Mena House, where the 1943 Cairo Declaration was agreed, with the Pyramids lit up next door.
Want the full story on the eclipse, the comet of 1882 and Egypt's North Korean connection? Dive into the Egypt eclipse blog.
The route
Cairo → Asyut → Abydos & Sohag → Tell el-Amarna → Giza
Hotels, most meals, a shaded desert eclipse camp, certified eclipse glasses, all transport and an Egyptologist guide are included. The day-by-day itinerary is on the booking page.
