The Big Question

Can I travel to North Korea?

The one question we get more than any other — and the key piece of information we’ve all been waiting for. The short answer:
Not yet.
North Korea is not currently open to tourism for the general public. Below is the latest and most up-to-date picture — all subject to change as soon as the powers-that-be decide to change their minds.

01  

Tourism in North Korea

Tourism to North Korea closed abruptly in January 2020. Since that time there have been very few opportunities to visit the country as a tourist — specifically, on a tourist visa.

   
Rason & tourism
The Rason area, in the far northeast, opened for a few weeks in early 2025.
 
   
Russian tourists
Since February 2024, the country has been open to tourists travelling on Russian passports. These tours visit fewer places and explore less extensively than any tourists could before 2020 — but they do offer access if you hold a specific passport.
 
   
General tourism now
For everyone else, there is currently no chance at present to visit North Korea as a tourist.
02  

When will North Korea open to tourism?

The second biggest question we get! Despite rumours, claims, and guesses to the contrary, North Korea remains closed to tourism. There are currently no opening dates established, no criteria for reopening announced, and no confirmed or reliable news available yet.

We at Koryo Tours are in constant contact with our partners in Pyongyang and around the country. As soon as there is any news, we will announce it on our website, on our social media channels, and to our newsletter mailing list — so you’re already in the right place.

 
“As soon as the DPRK opens again, we will resume our more than 30-year engagement with tours, trips, adventures, and more. Trust only those who know the place they are going — our record of access speaks for itself.”

03  

Other opportunities to visit North Korea

Tourism is not the only way to visit North Korea. There are also delegation visas, issued for various special events — and this has been a way in for some over the last couple of years.

An international trade fair welcomed some foreign visitors (after previous iterations were cancelled at the last minute), and the Pyongyang International Film Festival (PIFF) in 2025 featured films entered by foreign filmmakers — accompanied by their creators, alongside foreign jury members, whom Koryo Tours helped to find. It’s an option if you’re eligible: you need a film entered, accepted, and then to be invited.

04  

The Pyongyang Marathon

The 2025 Pyongyang Marathon saw around 200 amateur athletes enter the country. Sadly, the 2026 Marathon was cancelled. To take part, you simply need to be willing to run one of the races — anything from 5km to the full marathon. The chances are few, though, and the associated events have been somewhat unreliable.

The Pyongyang International Marathon remains the best bet while tourism is closed, and it will happen in 2027 — though the date and all details remain unconfirmed.

05  

North Korea business visas

These exist, and there have been visitors — mostly from China — entering the country on them in recent months. But they’re not available to people simply looking to visit on whatever visa they can get.

For a business visa you need to be invited by a company or organisation that is authorised to do so, that expects to do actual business with you, and that isn’t subject to the array of sanctions placed on North Korea by the outside world.

(Frankly and bluntly: this is a non-starter in almost all cases — sorry!)

06  

The overview

The current situation means that — aside from some delegation visas for special events, Russian passport holders, and business visas — travel to North Korea is generally not permitted. For the North Korea tourism industry, this is not great news.

But hopefully you now have the most up-to-date and clear information as we await updates, confirmations, and news of tourism resuming in some form. Stay with us and stay in touch — we sincerely hope you can travel to North Korea with Koryo Tours, on the best tours it is possible to take to the world’s most fascinating and complicated country… soon!
