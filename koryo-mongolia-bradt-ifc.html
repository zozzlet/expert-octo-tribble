01 — Before you open Canva

Set the canvas once and everything else falls into place

Watch this

The inside front cover is a left-hand page, so the binding runs down its right edge. Bradt asks for a further 10 mm of clearance from the right trim on an IFC. That means your usable text block is effectively 97 mm wide, not 107 — anything closer to the right disappears into the fold. Every mock-up below already respects it, which is why all three are weighted to the left.

In Canva: Custom size → 150 × 226 mm, then File → Settings → Show rulers & guides and drag guides to the numbers above. Export with Share → Download → PDF Print and tick Crop marks and bleed.

Image resolution at 300 dpi

  • Full-bleed background: ≥ 1772 × 2669 px
  • Photo across the text area: ≥ 1264 px wide
  • Half-width photo square: ≥ 620 px

Canva won't warn you loudly enough — check each photo before you place it, not after.

Two things to confirm with Bradt

  • Colour space. Canva exports RGB; most book printers want CMYK. Ask Harriet whether they'll convert, and expect the orange to shift slightly darker if they do.
  • Font embedding. PDF Print embeds fonts automatically — but if you use Circular Standard, make sure the licence covers embedding. Karla is open-licenced and safe.

Type — Karla throughout

  • Headline: ExtraBold, 26–34 pt, tracking −2.5%, line height 1.0
  • Sub / intro: Medium, 12–13 pt
  • Body: Regular, 9.5–10 pt, line height 1.4, ink at 85%
  • Labels & URL: Bold, 7–7.5 pt, tracking +18%, all caps

Circular Standard works identically if you prefer it — same sizes, drop tracking on the headline to −1.5%.

Motif rules

  • Squares only. No rounded corners, no circles except the logo mark.
  • Stripes appear once per page — either a bar or a row of small squares, never both.
  • Generous air. If it feels too empty, it's about right.
  • Ink, never black. #38434F for all type.

04 — Copy bank

Alternates, if none of the three headlines lands

Headlines

  • We don't visit Mongolia. We live there.
  • Where the road ends, we keep going.
  • Mongolia, from the inside.
  • Two decades. One country. No shortcuts.
  • The Naadam the coaches never reach.
  • Invited in, not driven past.

Short body — 25 to 40 words

  • Our Mongolia team lives in Mongolia. That's why Naadam finds us in the Altai with Kazakh eagle hunters and herding families, and why the plan shifts when the herders move. Small groups. Local guides. No script.
  • Twenty years of going back to the same valleys means the door opens differently. Naadam in the west, the Golden Eagle Festival, the Gobi — reached the slow way, with people who know the families at the other end.
Verify

Three claims in this copy are yours to confirm before it goes to print: the exact number of years in Mongolia, whether you want to publish a maximum group size, and whether to name a "from" price (the 2026 Naadam tour lists from US$2,950 — I'd leave prices off an inside cover, since the book will outlive the price).

  • Every photo ≥ 300 dpi at its placed size
  • Exported as PDF Print with crop marks and bleed
  • Colour space agreed with Bradt

Brand

  • Type is #38434F, not black, everywhere
  • One solid orange element, one stripe treatment
  • Official logo file placed — the lockups drawn here are stand-ins
  • URL correct: koryogroup.com/tours/mongolia
