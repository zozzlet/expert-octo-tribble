001  /  Why this eclipse

The big one. Literally.

On Monday 2 August 2027, the Moon's shadow sweeps across North Africa. It's the longest total eclipse you can see from easily accessible land this century, and Egypt gets the very best of it.
Most eclipse chasers will cram into Luxor. We're heading somewhere better: the desert near Asyut, deep inside the path of totality, in a part of Egypt few visitors ever see.
✓
Over six minutes of totality. Most total eclipses offer far less.
✓
The Sun almost directly overhead. No craning over a horizon.
✓
Almost zero chance of cloud. It's the Egyptian desert in August.
✓
Three nights in the path of totality. No dawn dash on eclipse morning.
002  /  Eclipse day, minute by minute

The moment the
lights go out

Morning
Into the desert
Transfer to our eclipse day camp: big shaded tent, cooling station, toilets, medical cover and our expert on hand all day.
11:35
First contact
Glasses on. The Moon takes its first bite. Over the next hour and a half the light turns strange and metallic, shadows sharpen and the air cools.
~1pm
Totality
Baily's beads, the diamond ring, then the corona blazes around a black Sun overhead. Venus and Mercury appear beside it, Jupiter on the other side. Over six minutes, glasses off.
Afternoon
Lunch & air con
Lunch at camp, then back to the hotel to compare photos and shake off the goosebumps.
Evening
Celebration dinner
A special Egyptian feast. Eclipses fall at new moon, so tonight is the darkest night of the month.

004  /  Only on a Koryo tour

Egypt's North Korean secret

You won't find this on any other Egypt itinerary. Day 6 is dedicated to one of the Cold War's strangest friendships.
✓
6th of October Panorama. Built in 1989 with North Korean artists and architects. A rotating hall around a giant battle painting, straight from the Pyongyang playbook.
✓
National Military Museum. Renovated by North Korean teams in the early 90s. Look closely at the faces in the dioramas…
✓
Egypt & the Two Koreas. A talk over Korean dinner in Zamalek: North Korean pilots in the 1973 war, Scud missiles, Mubarak's four Pyongyang visits and the Egyptian firm that built the DPRK's mobile network.
✓
The Cairo Declaration. On Day 7 we dine at Mena House, where Korea was promised its independence in 1943.

006  /  Good to know

Your questions, answered

How hot will it be?
Very. It's Egypt in August. We sightsee in the early mornings and spend the midday heat indoors, and the eclipse camp has shade and a cooling station. If you really struggle in the heat, this one may not be for you.
I'm travelling solo. Single room?
There are no classic single rooms in Asyut during eclipse week. Solo travellers get a private bedroom in a shared apartment with fellow travellers.
Can I see Luxor too?
Absolutely. Luxor, Aswan or a Nile cruise can be added as a private extension after the tour.
Anything else?
Days 4 and 5 involve long drives, and travel in Middle Egypt may need police escorts, so timings can shift. All part of the adventure.
