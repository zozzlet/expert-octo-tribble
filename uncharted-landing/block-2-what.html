01  What is Uncharted?

Forward motion

Koryo Uncharted is our new annual expedition. From ancient civilisations to extraordinary celestial events, each one is an entirely Koryo-curated itinerary you didn't know was possible, shared with our beloved Koryo community.

001

One-off

A complete one-time adventure built around something special, with exclusive itinerary items. Never to be repeated.

002

Voted by you

Every April our team pitches a handful of wild ideas. The shortlist goes to public vote, and you have the final say.

003

Tiny groups

Spaces are incredibly limited. Winning voters get first priority to book, then everyone who voted.
