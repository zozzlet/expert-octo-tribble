Part One · Days 1–9

Up the Indus — Karachi to Islamabad

An overland journey through 5,000 years of civilisation: from Pakistan's biggest port city, through the ancient cities and Sufi heartlands of Sindh and Punjab, to Mughal Lahore and the modern capital.

  Mohenjo-daro — walk the baked-brick streets of a 4,500-year-old UNESCO-listed city of the Indus Civilisation, whose writing is still undeciphered.
  Makli & Thatta — one of the world's largest historic burial grounds, plus Shah Jahan's blue-tiled mosque whose domes carry a whisper across the prayer hall.
  The Sufi south — the shrine of Lal Shahbaz Qalandar at Sehwan Sharif, the indigo-tiled tombs of Uch Sharif, and Multan, the City of Saints.
  Mughal Lahore — a full day in the Walled City: Wazir Khan Mosque, the Shahi Hammam, Lahore Fort and the Badshahi Mosque, ending with the theatrical Wagah Border ceremony.
  Katas Raj & Islamabad — Hindu temples around a sacred pond in the Salt Range, then the capital and Faisal Mosque beneath the Margalla Hills.

Part Two · Days 10–16

The Hindu Kush — Kalash Festival & Gandhara

Through the Lowari Tunnel into the mountains for the Chilam Joshi spring festival with the Kalasha community, then back south through Chitral and the Buddhist valleys of ancient Gandhara.

  Chilam Joshi — two days at the Kalasha spring festival: drums, group dances, embroidered dress and rituals welcoming the new season. A living community celebration, not a show staged for visitors — we attend as guests, on our hosts' terms.
  The Kalash Valleys — timber-and-stone villages above orchards and glacial streams in Bumburet, home to one of the world's smallest Indigenous ethnoreligious communities.
  Chitral — the former princely capital: fort, Shahi Mosque, bazaar of Chitrali caps and shawls, and nights at the famous Hindukush Heights hotel.
  Buddhist Swat — once ancient Uddiyana: the Shingardar Stupa, Butkara I sanctuary, the cliff-carved Jahanabad Buddha and the Swat Museum's Gandharan treasures.
  Takht-i-Bahi — the UNESCO-listed ridge-top monastery near Mardan, occupied for seven centuries, before a farewell dinner in Islamabad.

Good to know
