THE TOUR CARD
-------------
Built to match the website card in your screenshot: cream panel, burnt
ombre eyebrow date, burnt ombre title, the short ombre rule, the grey
subtitle, description, price, then a divided footer with a red BOOK NOW
and an ombre-outlined TOUR DETAILS.

One deliberate difference: the website card puts the image beside the text
in two columns. At 600px of email width that would leave roughly 270px per
column, and because images cannot stretch to match a text column's height
in email, the two sides would not line up. So the image sits on top
instead and the text runs full width beneath it. Everything else — the
colours, the rule, the two-button footer — is as per the site.

On mobile the two buttons stack full-width.

DESIGN NOTES
------------
Colour split is roughly 75% white / 20% burnt ombre / 5% other Koryo
colours.

  Burnt ombre    #BF750C   headings, eyebrows, stat panel, both Find Out
                           More buttons, tour card title and rule, sign-up
  Cream          #FAF0E6   tour card, Eratosthenes panel, field guide panel
  Cream hairline #EBD9C0   card borders, reading-list numerals
  Red            #E8112D   stripe, BOOK NOW button, Cyrene rule
  Teal           #45B5AA   stripe, Apollonia rule
  Orange         #F49B1B   stripe
  Purple         #3D3A8C   stripe, Qasr Libya rule
  Green          #2E9B4C   stripe

The Koryo stripe leads with burnt ombre rather than red, so the email
reads as Libya at a glance — same approach as the Bhutan newsletters,
which lead with teal.

Fonts are Circular Standard for headings and Karla for body, with
Helvetica/Arial fallbacks.

Dark mode via prefers-color-scheme plus Outlook.com's [data-ogsc] /
[data-ogsb] hooks. The cream panels invert to a warm dark brown (#2A2118)
rather than going neutral grey, so the Libya identity survives; burnt
ombre brightens to #E8A34A so it stays legible.

Mobile breakpoint 640px. 600px wide, ~41KB — well under Gmail's 102KB
clipping limit.


THE FOUR PIECES
---------------
All four are markdown, all comfortably over the 1,500-word brief, all
written in Koryo voice. They cross-link to each other and each closes with
a short line about the tour.

Piece 3 (the planning guide) opens with an editor's note. Please read it.
The visa, permit and flight sections describe how Libya travel works in
general terms and how you handle it — but koryogroup.com was unreachable
from this environment, so none of it is checked against your live tour
page. Confirm the specifics with operations before publishing, and delete
the note once you have.

Facts in piece 1 are sourced and checked: 6m23s at greatest eclipse,
~6m9s at Benghazi, 253km path width, landfall ~09:30 UT, Saros 136
(#38 of 71), longest from land between 1991 and 2114.

One honest adjustment worth knowing about: the carousel says "there isn't
another in your lifetime." That is very nearly true but not quite — the
12 August 2045 eclipse over the United States runs to about six minutes,
just short of 2027. Piece 1 names it rather than skating past it, on the
grounds that an eclipse audience will know, and being the ones who
mentioned it is better than being the ones who did not.


ASSETS FROM YOUR DECKS
----------------------
Only two bitmap images are actually embedded in the two .pptx files — the
phase strip and the corona roundel. Everything else in the carousel (the
path map, the panels, the type) is drawn as native PowerPoint vector
shapes, so there is nothing to extract.

To get the rest as images: open the deck in PowerPoint and use
File > Export > Change File Type > PNG, then "All Slides". That gives you
fourteen ready-made social assets, and slides 3, 4, 7 and 13 in particular
would drop straight into this email if you want it more visual.

(LibreOffice was available here and would normally do that conversion
headlessly, but it refused to parse these two files, so this step is
yours.)

----------
