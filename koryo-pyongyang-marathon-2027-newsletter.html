Pyongyang Marathon 2027:
What We Know

No big news yet. But you ask us about it every single day, so here’s exactly where things stand.

Here at Koryo Tours Towers, one question comes up more than almost any other: when is the Pyongyang Marathon 2027? When will it be confirmed? How do I book?

Sadly, we can’t give you satisfying answers just yet, because the event hasn’t been confirmed. But since it gets so much interest (and so much speculation), we want to share exactly what we know, what we don’t, and what we’re all waiting for.

A little background

How we got here

2014
The first year foreign amateur runners could enter, with Koryo Tours bringing them in.
2014 to 2019
Numbers kept growing, up to around 900 visiting runners alongside a similar number of locals. Trips ran from 2 nights to 2 weeks+, by plane or train.
2020
Everything stopped. The DPRK closed to most visitors, and tourism is still on hold today.
2025
The marathon came back! Around 200 runners, all invited as amateur athletes, on 5-night Pyongyang packages.
2026
Confirmed in December 2025, then cancelled by the DPRK side a month before race day, “due to some reasons”.
2027
Expected, but not yet confirmed. This is where we are now.

The race itself has been running for decades. Before Covid, trips were far more flexible, with more travel options around the country and much more capacity. We hope it will be that way again, but that needs tourism to reopen, which we’re all still waiting for.

Your questions, our answers

What we know so far

We speak to the Pyongyang Marathon Committee very often. But they don’t make the final call on dates or confirmation, so they’re waiting too.
 

When will it happen?

No date has been fixed. Traditionally the race was on the second Sunday in April. In 2025 it moved to the first Sunday in April, and 2026 was planned the same way. For 2027? No information yet.

There’s also some talk it could move later in the year. The race used to be called the Mangyongdae Prize International Marathon, tied to Kim Il Sung’s birthday on 15 April. Now it’s the Pyongyang International Marathon, so April may no longer be a must.
 

How many people can take part?

Up to 2019, any tourist in the country at the time could run. In 2025 only invited guests of the DPRK Athletics Association could take part, and hosting capacity was more limited. 2027 could be a couple of hundred people or several hundred. We think it will be more than 2025, as more flights (and even trains) are running again. Either way, we’ll fit in as many of you as we possibly can.
 

When will it be confirmed?

The big one! Honestly? Nobody knows. The 2026 race was confirmed in early December 2025. The 2025 race was confirmed only about 5 weeks out. Before 2019 it was months in advance. So anything is possible. We ask for updates often, and we’ll pass news on the moment we have it. We know it’s frustrating. Sorry!
 

Who can and can’t take part?

Not fully clear yet, and it may depend on whether tourism has reopened. What we do know: South Korean citizens can’t go, and US and Malaysian passport holders are restricted by their own governments. Japanese passport holders are unlikely to be accepted, and US dual citizens may be restricted too (not confirmed). There will likely also be health-related restrictions for people from, or who have recently visited, countries with outbreaks like Ebola. Final details are up to the DPRK side.
