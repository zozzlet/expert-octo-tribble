Tour One • 7 Days

Independence Day in East Timor

A week in Dili and a night on the reef. We learn how independence was won, meet the people rebuilding the country, then join thousands of Timorese for the 25th anniversary celebrations.
Day 1
Arrive in Dili, welcome dinner and briefing
Day 2
Sunrise Cristo Rei hike, Tais Market, Dare Memorial, Motael Church and Santa Cruz Cemetery
Day 3
A conversation with a resistance survivor, the Chega! exhibition, Alola Foundation and Ba Futuru
Day 4
Barge to Atauro Island, reef briefing, women's craft cooperatives and a beach BBQ
Day 5
Dawn snorkelling, island market, ferry back to Dili for the Independence Eve mass
Day 6
Independence Day: flag-raising, parade, street celebrations and a farewell dinner
Day 7
Tour ends in Dili

Tour Two • 12 Days

Travel East Timor & West Timor

Everything in the short tour, then a charter flight to the Oecusse enclave and overland across the border into Indonesian West Timor, where we spend a night in Boti, the last kingdom of Timor.
Day 1-6
Same as the short tour, Independence Day included
Day 7
Charter flight from Dili to Oecusse, Lifau Monument and the colonial waterfront
Day 8
Oesilo mud volcanoes, Pante Makassar beach and the abandoned ZEESM buildings
Day 9
Overland through the Wini border into West Timor and up to Boti Village
Day 10
Morning with the King of Boti, then down to Kupang via Camplong and Oebelo
Day 11
Crystal Cave, Kupang Museum, Fort Concordia and a farewell dinner at Cafe Inklusi
Day 12
Tour ends in Kupang
Heads up: max 8 travellers. The charter to Oecusse is a small plane planned by weight, so this tour is capped at 8 people.
