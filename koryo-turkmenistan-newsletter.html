Where it all started

Before the tourists, there was Simon

Back in 2004, our very own Simon Cockerell hopped on one of the first ever flights from Beijing to Ashgabat. The plan? Have a good look around and see if this famously closed country could be opened up to travellers like you.

Turns out, it could. In May 2006 our first group tour set off. Back then a tourism industry in Turkmenistan barely existed, so we rolled up our sleeves and helped build one, laying a path that plenty of others have since followed.

Twenty years on, a lot has changed. Ashgabat keeps growing, some of the old landmarks have gone quiet (the Ruhnama Monument now just sits there, inert), and the country even closed its doors for a few years before welcoming visitors back. Through all of it, we’ve kept going back. Simon was there again in October 2024, camping by the crater and meeting eagle hunters out in the Karakum Desert.

Simon has written up the whole story, odd moments and all. It’s a cracking read.
