Part One · Days 1–7

The Hadhramaut — mainland Yemen

An epic road trip from Seiyun through the mud-brick civilisation of the Hadhramaut valley to the Arabian Sea coast — police escort throughout, the best available hotels, and a complimentary traditional Yemeni outfit to wear along the way.

[ IMAGE — Shibam towers, 250×170 ]
[ IMAGE — Wadi Doan canyon, 250×170 ]
  Shibam — the ‘Manhattan of the Desert’. 500 mud-brick towers up to 40 metres tall, UNESCO-listed, sunset over the skyline.
  Tarim & Qabr Hud — the world's tallest mud-brick minaret, and a desert pilgrimage town deserted for all but three days a year.
  Wadi Doan — two nights at Haid Al-Jazil Resort, perched on a boulder 150m above the valley floor. Infinity pool included.
  Sidr honey tasting with a local beekeeper — prized across the Arab world — plus home-cooked meals and village coffee stops.
  Al-Mukalla — white buildings and minarets on the Arabian Sea, ending with the famous dawn fish market.

Part Two · Days 7–13

Socotra — the alien island

A full island circuit by 4x4, camping on beaches and dragon blood forest plateaus under some of the clearest night skies on Earth. Over a third of Socotra's plants exist nowhere else — and a local team of chefs cooks three fresh meals a day while you soak it all up.

[ IMAGE — Dragon blood trees, 250×170 ]
[ IMAGE — Detwah Lagoon, 250×170 ]
  Detwah Lagoon — first night camped beside a turquoise lagoon ringed by limestone mountains. Morning swims before breakfast.
  Dixam Plateau & Fermahin Forest — the densest dragon blood tree forests on the island, plus traditional resin harvesting at Homhil.
  Zahek Dunes & Arher Beach — golden dunes tumbling into the sea, freshwater springs running across the sand, bonfire dinner on the beach.
  Hoq Cave & Kalisan Canyon — a vast stalactite cave, and hidden freshwater pools at the foot of towering cliffs.
  Dihamri Marine Protected Area — the finest snorkelling in the Arabian Sea: coral gardens, turtles, octopus and more. Then a grand finale in Aden.
