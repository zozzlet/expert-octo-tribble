Moldova & Transnistria

Deep Into the Two Republics
Seven nights across Moldova, Gagauzia and the breakaway state of Transnistria. Europe's most overlooked corner, seen properly for once.

Fancy a European trip with a proper difference? We're heading into the least-visited part of the continent: Moldova and the breakaway state of Transnistria, with the semi-autonomous region of Gagauzia in between. This isn't a whirlwind capital-to-capital trip, we go deep into all three to get under the skin of what makes each one tick.

Quality accommodation, characterful meals, free evenings to explore, and the company of a Koryo Tours leader throughout. Starts and ends in Chisinau, Moldova's capital.

The Best Bits

Three places, one week, no filler

Chisinau
Architecture & wine
Moldova's capital, a striking mix of Soviet, imperial and modern architecture, plus wineries and villages just outside the city.
 
Gagauzia
Turkic & autonomous
A semi-autonomous Turkic region of Moldova with its own language, traditions and a warmth all of its own.
 
Transnistria
North, south & Tiraspol
The breakaway PMR, north and south, plus its time capsule capital: the most complete look at this remarkable unrecognised state you'll find anywhere.
Please note the itinerary may shift slightly on the day, your tour leader will always add extras when there's time, whether that's a local bar or a passing funfair.

Day By Day

The full seven nights

DAY 1 · 4 May
Chisinau
Arrive and settle in, then a walking tour through Soviet-era and imperial architecture, parks, monuments and churches. Overnight Chisinau.
 
DAY 2 · 5 May
Into Gagauzia
Manuc Bey Mansion, then across into Gagauzia for the Gagauz history museum and a first taste of this distinct culture. Overnight Comrat.
 
DAY 3 · 6 May
Comrat & wine
Walking tour of Gagauzia's capital, a well-preserved village, and lunch and tasting at Vinuri de Comrat winery. Back to Chisinau for a free evening.
 
DAY 4 · 7 May
Cross the border
Cricova, 128km of wine tunnels beneath Chisinau, then Curchi Monastery, then we cross into Transnistria. Overnight Dubasari.
 
DAY 5 · 8 May
North Transnistria
Rabnita's steel mill and Soviet mosaics, Rascov village, Camenca, and monuments most visitors never see. Overnight Tiraspol.
 
DAY 6 · 9 May
Victory Day
A full Tiraspol city tour plus the Victory Day celebrations themselves, then Bender Fortress, dating back to the 16th century. Overnight Tiraspol.
 
DAY 7 · 10 May
South Transnistria
Sturgeon and caviar at Aquatir, small towns along the Dniester, and the Noul Neamt Monastery. Final night in Tiraspol.
 
DAY 8 · 11 May
Departure
Back over the border to Chisinau to fly home, or carry on your travels onward, including by train into Romania.
