  - "still, well, straight" — credited in the email as "as our blog puts
    it", so it reads as a nod to the piece rather than a lift

One thing I corrected against your text: my first draft had the Umayyad
Mosque starting life as a temple to Hadad. Your post says Roman temple to
Jupiter in Saul's day, then Byzantine basilica of St John the Baptist in
the late fourth century, then the mosque built 705–715 under al-Walid I.
The email now follows your version, including the detail that Christians
and Muslims shared the site for a period after 636 — which is the most
striking fact in that section.

I also kept your caveat on the Minaret of Jesus: the email says Islamic
tradition holds that Jesus will descend there, and then states plainly
that the Bible itself makes no such connection. Worth keeping — it is the
sort of precision that earns trust with this audience.

The personal framing is there too, lightly: the line before the blog
button mentions a colleague who grew up in a devout Catholic family and
came back to these stories through the Middle East trips. Change "our
colleague" to a name if you would rather credit the author directly.

THE CHRISTMAS TOUR
------------------
Featured, and visually senior to the second card: 2px yellow border rather
than 1px cream, a solid yellow Tour Details button rather than an outlined
one, and a red "ONLY 4 PLACES LEFT" chip above the title.

The bridge into it is the section headed "Christmas on the street called

crucifixion, Ananias was already living there when Paul arrived, and Bab

whole reason the Christmas departure is worth featuring on this particular
newsletter, so it is stated before the card rather than inside it.

DESIGN NOTES
------------
Colour split is roughly 75% white / 20% yellow / 5% other Koryo colours —
the same yellow system as the Iraq newsletter, so the two sit together as
a Middle East pair.

  Yellow         #F0B429   quote panel, blog button, featured card border
                           and button, rules, stripe
  Deep gold      #9A6F06   eyebrows, links, second card's button
  Cream          #FEF7E4   mosque panel, both tour cards
  Cream hairline #F2E3BC   card borders
  Red            #E8112D   stripe, Naaman quote, "4 places left" chip
  Teal           #45B5AA   stripe, the scales quote
  Purple         #3D3A8C   stripe, Isaiah quote
  Green          #2E9B4C   stripe, the basket quote, Maaloula

Yellow never carries text on white — it is a background with near-black
text (#2A1F02) on it, and deep gold does the work anywhere yellow would
have been a text colour. Koryo orange is left out of the stripe here, as
on the Iraq email, because it reads too close to this yellow.

Fonts are Circular Standard for headings and Karla for body. Scripture is
italic Karla rather than a serif, since the brief is those two families.

Dark mode via prefers-color-scheme plus Outlook.com's [data-ogsc] /
[data-ogsb] hooks. Cream panels invert to a warm dark brown; gold
brightens to #FFD166. The yellow quote panel stays yellow in both modes,
deliberately — it is the loudest thing in the email.

Mobile breakpoint 640px. 600px wide, ~46KB, well under Gmail's 102KB
clipping limit.
